M'Trade management

Coordinate the path from trade opportunity to finance-ready submission.

M'Trade brings the transaction journey, people, requirements, documents, actions and status into one governed workflow — so teams can address gaps before presenting the case to a financing partner.

01

Opportunity

Record the trade need and intended outcome.

02

Counterparties

Coordinate buyers, sellers and relevant parties.

03

Requirements

Map conditions, documents and responsibilities.

04

Readiness

Identify gaps before submission.

05

Submission

Present an organised transaction package.

06

Monitoring

Track follow-up, status and obligations.

FOR BUSINESSES

Understand what the transaction needs, assign the work and maintain a coherent readiness record.

FOR ADVISERS

Coordinate the client journey, requirements and evidence without losing context across hand-offs.

FOR FINANCING PARTNERS

Receive a better-organised case with clearer ownership, status and supporting information.

Important governance boundary: M'Project supports preparation, coordination and monitoring. Banks and other authorised institutions retain KYC/AML review, compliance assessment, credit decisions, pricing, approval and the provision of finance.
